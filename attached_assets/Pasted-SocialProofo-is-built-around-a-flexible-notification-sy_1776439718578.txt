SocialProofo is built around a flexible notification system designed to influence user behavior through real-time or simulated activity cues. The platform provides a wide variety of on-site notifications such as recent sales popups, live visitor counters, conversion alerts, email subscription prompts, countdown timers, and review or rating messages. These notifications are displayed dynamically on a website to create urgency and credibility, encouraging users to take immediate action. Each notification type can be configured to appear based on specific triggers such as time delay, scroll position, exit intent, or user inactivity, allowing precise control over when and how messages are shown.

The system also supports detailed customization of notification appearance and behavior. Users can modify text, colors, positioning, animations, and display frequency to match their website design and marketing strategy. Notifications can be targeted to specific pages, devices, or audience segments, ensuring relevance and avoiding unnecessary repetition. Additionally, the platform allows the use of custom HTML notifications, enabling advanced users to create completely unique designs or integrate third-party elements within the notification framework.

Another important aspect is the ability to simulate activity when real data is not available. This includes generating randomized or rule-based notifications that mimic user interactions, helping new or low-traffic websites maintain a sense of engagement. At the same time, real-time data can be integrated where available to display genuine user actions, increasing authenticity.

SocialProofo also includes built-in analytics specifically for notifications, tracking impressions, clicks, and conversion rates for each campaign. This allows users to evaluate which notification types and configurations perform best and optimize accordingly. Overall, the notification system is designed to be highly adaptable, combining behavioral triggers, visual customization, and performance tracking to improve user engagement and conversion rates without requiring complex development work.
